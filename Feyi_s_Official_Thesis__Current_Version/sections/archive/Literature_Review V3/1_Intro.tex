SoTS emerge at the intersection of SoS and DTs. As these areas converge, more work is beginning to describe systems where multiple DTs interact, coordinate, and evolve following SoS principles.  This chapter presents a systematic literature review (SLR) of SoTS, based on the study design in \secref{sec:literature-review-study-design}. A classification framework, introduced in \secref{sec:literature-review-classification}, is used to identify common architectural patterns in SoTS. The results are presented in \secref{sec:literature-review-results} and summarized into key takeaways in \secref{sec:literature-review-key-takeaways}. Related work is discussed in \secref{sec:literature-review-related-work}.
