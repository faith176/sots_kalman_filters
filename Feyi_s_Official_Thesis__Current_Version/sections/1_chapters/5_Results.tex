\chapter{Results}\label{ch:results}
This chapter presents the experimental evaluation of the proposed architecture. Building on the design detailed in Chapter~\ref{ch:architecture}, this chapter assesses the system’s ability to maintain event reasoning continuity and correctness when constituent systems intermittently leave or rejoin the SoTS.  

The chapter is organized in two main parts. The first part (\secref{sec:results-eval}) introduces the evaluation scenario, outlining the problem formulation, baseline configurations, simulation environment, and quantitative metrics used to assess performance. The second part (\secref{sec:results-res}) presents and analyzes the experimental findings, comparing the proposed architecture against baseline configurations.
