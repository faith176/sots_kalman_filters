SoTS emerge at the intersection of SoS and DTs. As these areas converge, more work is beginning to describe systems where multiple DTs interact, coordinate, and evolve following SoS principles. Such systems appear across multiple domains. Examples include smart cities, where DTs of infrastructure, vehicles, and urban services interact \cite{human2023design,somma2023digital}. 
They also include manufacturing systems composed of machines, robots, and production lines \cite{gill2022method,gollner2022collaborative}, as well as autonomous systems such as fleets of vehicles or robots \cite{barden2022academic,novak2022digitalized}. This chapter presents a systematic literature review (SLR) of SoTS, based on the study design described in \secref{sec:literature-review-study-design}. A classification framework, introduced in \secref{sec:literature-review-classification}, is used to identify common architectural patterns in SoTS. The results are presented in \secref{sec:literature-review-results} and summarized into key takeaways in \secref{sec:literature-review-key-takeaways}. Related work is discussed in \secref{sec:literature-review-related-work}.
