SoTS arise at the intersection of SoS and DTs. As these research directions converge, a growing body of work describes systems in which multiple digitally twinned systems interact, coordinate, and evolve under SoS principles. This chapter presents a systematic literature review conducted according to the study design described in \secref{sec:literature-review-study-design}. To support the analysis, a classification framework for SoTS (\secref{sec:literature-review-classification}) is introduced and used to identify and compare recurring architectural patterns and coordination strategies across the literature. The results are presented in \secref{sec:literature-review-results} and synthesized into key takeaways in \secref{sec:literature-review-key-takeaways}, which ground the problem addressed in subsequent chapters. Related work is discussed in \secref{sec:literature-review-related-work}.
