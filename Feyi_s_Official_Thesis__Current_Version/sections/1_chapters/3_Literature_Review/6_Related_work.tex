\section{Related work}\label{sec:literature-review-related-work}
% For additional readings, the reader is referred to studies related to ours, as detailed below.

Several studies acknowledge that DTs can be composed into SoS and that such integration can provide system-level benefits. \citet{olsson2023systems-of-systems} reviews ten studies combining DTs and SoS and identifies challenges related to interoperability and lifecycle management. \citet{mylonas2021digital} survey DT applications in manufacturing and smart cities and examine integration patterns, while \citet{semeraro2021digital} identify twinning cases at both unit and system levels. \citet{bottjer2023review} focus on manufacturing DTs, situating them within hierarchical and SoS structures. \citet{bertoni2022designing} argue that understanding lifecycle value and emergent behaviour requires connecting multiple DTs into higher-order configurations. \citet{dalibor2022cross-domain} identify SoS as a class of systems that can be digitally twinned, and \citet{klar2023digital} position interoperability across DTs as the highest level of DT maturity. Despite these benefits, engineering DT-enabled SoS remains challenging due to interoperability gaps, connectivity and privacy concerns, limited standardization, and the operational independence of constituent systems \cite{michael2022integration,dietz2020digital,borth2019digital,gill2024automating}.
