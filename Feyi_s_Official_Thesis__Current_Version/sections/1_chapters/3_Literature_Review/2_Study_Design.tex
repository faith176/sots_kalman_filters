\section{Study Design}\label{sec:literature-review-study-design}
This review followed established systematic literature review guidelines~\cite{kitchenham2007guidelines, wohlin2020guidelines} to ensure methodological rigor, transparency, and reproducibility. The research questions shaped the identification of relevant studies, the extraction of evidence, and the synthesis of findings regarding the integration of Digital Twins and Systems of Systems.

A structured search across Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, and the ACM Digital Library produced 317 publications. After duplicate removal and screening using predefined inclusion and exclusion criteria, 196 unique studies remained. Full-text screening and quality assessment, conducted independently by two reviewers, reduced this to 53 primary studies. Forward and backward snowballing added 27 more, yielding a final set of \textbf{80 primary studies}.

\subsection{Planning and Scope Definition}\label{subsec:literature-review-study-phases-planning}
The review protocol defined the scope, research questions, search terms, and quality criteria prior to data collection. Searches targeted peer-reviewed Computer Science and Engineering publications and combined Digital Twin and System-of-Systems terminology, including common variants identified during preliminary exploration. The final search query set included expressions such as \textit{``digital twin*'' AND ``system* of systems''}, as well as synonymous forms (e.g., \textit{``aggregated digital twin*''}, \textit{``system of digital twins''}, \textit{``digital twin of systems''}, and \textit{``system* of twinned systems''}).

\subsection{Database Search and Screening}\label{subsec:literature-review-study-phases-screening}
Screening was performed using exclusion criteria addressing accessibility, relevance, and the presence of both DT and SoS concepts. Titles and abstracts were evaluated by two reviewers independently, with disagreements resolved through discussion to ensure consistency and reduce selection bias. \tabref{tab:literature_review_search_statistics} summarizes the full search, screening, and exclusion statistics across all stages of the automated search phase, including inter-rater agreement and reliability metrics.

\subsection{Selection and Quality Assessment}\label{subsec:literature-review-study-phases-selection}
Candidate studies were evaluated using four quality dimensions: clarity of SoS description, clarity of DT description, tangibility of contributions, and reporting quality. Studies that did not meet minimum thresholds were excluded, resulting in 53 high-quality papers from the initial automated search.

\subsection{Snowballing}\label{subsec:literature-review-study-phases-snowballing}
Backward and forward snowballing followed the recommendations of \citet{wohlin2020guidelines}. The same screening and quality criteria were applied to referenced and citing works. This process identified 27 additional relevant studies, complementing the initial search and ensuring broader coverage of the research landscape.

\subsection{Data Extraction and Synthesis}\label{subsec:literature-review-study-phases-extraction}
Data were extracted using a structured coding framework addressing conceptual alignment, architectural and modelling practices, treatment of reliability and other non-functional properties, research and technological maturity, and implementation choices. Synthesis used both quantitative and qualitative techniques~\cite{franzosi2010quantitative, rodgers2009testing}. Classification was supported by established taxonomies for SoS~\cite{nielsen2015systems}, DTs~\cite{kritzinger2018digital, david2024infonomics}, and dependability~\cite{avizienis2004basic}. All extraction scripts and aggregated datasets are included in the replication package.\footnote{\url{https://github.com/ssm-lab/system-of-twinned-systems-replication-package}}
