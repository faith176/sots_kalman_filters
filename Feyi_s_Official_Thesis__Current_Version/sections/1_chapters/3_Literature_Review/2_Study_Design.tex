\section{Study Design}\label{sec:literature-review-study-design}

The review is guided by seven research questions that collectively examine the motivations, structure, characteristics, and maturity of SoTS. Specifically, we investigate:

\rquestion{RQ1. \ul{Why} are DT and SoS principles applied jointly?}
We investigate the purposes, problems, and domains of SoTS.

\rquestion{RQ2. \ul{How} are DT and SoS combined?}
We identify architectures for organizing DTs within SoS, including the nature of constituent units, and the type of SoTS.

\rquestion{RQ3. What are the \ul{technical characteristics of DTs} in SoTS?}
We examine DT properties such as autonomy, services, and modeling approaches.

\rquestion{RQ4. What are the \ul{technical characteristics of SoS} in SoTS?}
We analyze SoS properties, including support for key dimensions, and the types of emergent behaviour considered.

\rquestion{RQ5. How are \ul{non-functional properties} addressed in SoTS?}
We focus on reliability (continuity of correct service~\cite{avizienis2004basic}) and security, examining whether these are addressed architecturally, explicitly modeled, or empirically evaluated.

\rquestion{RQ6. What is the level of \ul{technical and research maturity} in SoTS?}
Technical maturity is assessed using Technology Readiness Levels (TRL), ranging from initial concepts to operational systems \cite{cmankins2009technology}. Research maturity is evaluated based on study validation approaches \cite{petersen2015guidelines}, including the use of standards.

\rquestion{RQ7. What are the typical \ul{technological choices} to implement SoTS?}
We analyze the use of programming languages, frameworks, and platforms.

This review followed established guidelines for systematic literature reviews~\cite{kitchenham2007guidelines, wohlin2020guidelines} to ensure rigor and reproducibility. A structured search was conducted across Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, and the ACM Digital Library using combinations of DT and SoS terminology. Core expressions included \textit{“digital twin*” AND “system* of systems”} alongside common variants identified during preliminary exploration (e.g., \textit{“aggregated digital twin*”}, \textit{“system of digital twins”}, and \textit{“digital twin of systems”}). This initial search yielded 317 publications. After duplicate removal and screening titles and abstracts using predefined inclusion and exclusion criteria, 196 studies remained for full-text review. Quality assessment---evaluating clarity of DT and SoS descriptions, tangibility of contributions, and reporting quality---narrowed this set to 53 high-quality primary studies. The overall workflow is summarized in \figref{fig:literature_review_study_design_overview}.

To broaden coverage, forward and backward snowballing was performed following \citet{wohlin2020guidelines}, applying the same screening and quality criteria to referenced and citing works. This process added 27 additional studies, producing a final set of \textbf{80 primary papers}. Screening was conducted by two reviewers, with disagreements resolved through discussion, and \tabref{tab:literature_review_search_statistics} summarizes the search and screening outcomes.

Data extraction followed a structured coding process addressing conceptual alignment, architectural and modelling practices, treatment of non-functional properties, research maturity, and implementation choices. Synthesis combined quantitative and qualitative techniques~\cite{franzosi2010quantitative, rodgers2009testing} and was guided by established SoS~\cite{nielsen2015systems}, DT~\cite{kritzinger2018digital, david2024infonomics}, and dependability~\cite{avizienis2004basic} taxonomies. \subsection{Threats to validity}\label{sec:literature-review-threats}
Our observations depend on the selected studies and may be affected by selection bias, missed publications, and limitations in search terminology due to the lack of a unified definition of SoTS. To mitigate these threats, we followed established empirical guidelines~\cite{wohlin2020guidelines}, using trusted databases, validated selection procedures, and snowballing to extend the corpus~\cite{greenhalgh2005effectiveness}, along with expanded search terms. Manual quality assessment introduces subjectivity; this was mitigated through regular discussions to align interpretations and ensure consistent application of the criteria.
