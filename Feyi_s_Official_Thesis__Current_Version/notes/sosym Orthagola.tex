\subsubsection{SoTS types vs distribution}
\label{sec:orthogonal-sots-distribution}

We observed a statistically significant relationship between SoTS architectural type and support for the SoS distribution property ($\chi^2 = 13.043$, $p = 0.04236$). \figref{fig:sots-sos-heatmap} shows that support for distribution is prevalent across all SoTS types. All Directed SoTS support distribution (\xofy{26}{26}), as do most Acknowledged SoTS (\xofy{27}{31}). Collaborative SoTS also largely support distribution (\xofy{17}{19}), while all Virtual SoTS in the sample do so (\xofy{4}{4}). In contrast, other SoS dimensions do not exhibit the same level of consistent support across architectural types. Properties such as evolution, reconfiguration, and emergence show greater variation and lower overall coverage. This concentration of statistical significance on distribution suggests that SoTS architectures consistently address spatial and organizational separation of constituents, while other SoS characteristics are treated more selectively.




\subsubsection{Application domain vs SoS dimensions}\label{sec:orthogonal-domain-sos}

We analyzed the relationship between application domain and support for individual SoS dimensions using chi-square tests of independence. Statistically significant relationships were observed for some SoS properties.Domain and support for SoS distribution exhibit a statistically significant relationship ($p = 1.975e{-05}$). Distribution is widely supported across all domains, and this significance reflects its near-universal inclusion in SoTS. Similarly, a significant relationship was found between domain and SoS interoperability ($p = 0.002773$), indicating that while interoperability is broadly addressed, its degree of support varies across application contexts. In contrast, the relationship between application domain and SoS evolution is only marginally significant ($\chi^2 = 40.571$, $p = 0.09427$). This suggests that limited support for evolutionary development is consistently observed across domains, with no strong evidence that any particular application area consistently addresses evolution at the SoTS level.
