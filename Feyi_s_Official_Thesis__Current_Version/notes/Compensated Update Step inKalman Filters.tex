% Kalman Filters dealing with missing data in the update step
liu2004kalman - study how Kalman filters behave when some sensor data is randomly lost during transmission. They show that the filter's stability depends on how often each sensor’s data is successfully received, and they provide conditions to keep the estimation error bounded. The paper also explains how to handle updates when only partial measurements are available, by treating missing values as high variance noise and uses the avaliable measurements only. When all observations are lost it performs an open ended predict

jin2022distributed - This paper addresses the problem of distributed state estimation in multi-sensor systems with randomly missing data due to unreliable sensing or communication. The authors propose three distributed Kalman filter variants that incorporate missing data indicators to determine whether local or neighbor information can be used. When data is available, each sensor fuses its own measurements with delayed or current estimates from neighbors using a Linear Unbiased Minimum Variance (LUMV) fusion strategy. The paper proves the stability and steady-state convergence of the proposed filters and validates their performance through simulation.

shi2010kalman - This paper proposes a Kalman filter-based recursive algorithm for system identification in networked environments with randomly missing input and output measurements. The missing data is modeled as two independent Bernoulli processes, and a lightweight output estimator is introduced to reconstruct lost measurements. The identification algorithm fuses observed data with estimated outputs using modified Kalman gain updates, enabling parameter and output estimation despite incomplete observations. The authors provide rigorous convergence analysis and validate the method’s effectiveness through simulations under varying data loss scenarios.

naik2024kalman - This paper presents a modified Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) for identifying multiple sinusoids from superimposed signals when measurements are intermittently missing. The authors reformulate the EKF's measurement model using Bernoulli variables to account for random missing data and derive new update equations for measurement estimates, covariances, and Kalman gain. The resulting EKF variant (EKF_M) maintains stability under bounded noise and initial errors, and significantly outperforms traditional Gaussian filters in estimation accuracy, even with up to 60\% missing data. Simulations confirm that EKF_M offers both improved accuracy and computational efficiency, making it suitable for high-frequency applications and general filtering tasks.

khan2010improvement - This paper introduces a Compensated Closed-Loop Kalman Filter (CCLKF) that improves state estimation for discrete-time LTI systems when sensor measurements are intermittently lost. The approach reconstructs missing observations using linear prediction based on past data, then feeds them into the Kalman update step to maintain estimation accuracy. Compared to open-loop methods, CCLKF significantly reduces estimation error and avoids instability during and after data loss. Simulation on a mass-spring-damper system shows that CCLKF outperforms conventional and open-loop Kalman filters, though at the cost of higher computation and memory requirements.
