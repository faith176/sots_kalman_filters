\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{I. Analytical and Modeling-Oriented Strategies}} \\
\midrule

\textbf{Runtime Monitoring and Verification} 
& Detect, diagnose, and verify disturbances during execution. 
& Fault Detection and Isolation (FDI); runtime verification (e.g., LTL/CTL monitors); behavioral deviation detection; anomaly detection \\

\textbf{Dependency and Graph-Based Modeling} 
& Represent structural interdependencies and failure propagation paths across constituents. 
& Functional Dependency Network Analysis (FDNA); graph and multi-graph models; bond graph models; failure propagation models \\

\textbf{Probabilistic and Stochastic Modeling} 
& Quantify reliability under uncertainty and evolving configurations. 
& Bayesian Networks; Markov chains and Continuous-Time Markov Models; Petri nets; Monte Carlo simulation \\

\textbf{Structured Hazard and Risk Analysis} 
& Systematically identify and analyze potential failure modes and hazards. 
& Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA); Failure Mode, Effects and Criticality Analysis (FMECA); Fault Tree Analysis (FTA); Event Tree Analysis; Hazard and Operability Study (HAZOP) \\

\midrule
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textbf{II. Compensation and Adaptation Strategies}} \\
\midrule

\textbf{Redundancy and Substitution} 
& Preserve service using pre-engineered alternative components or capabilities. 
& Substitutable Heterogeneous System Architectures (SHSA); stand-in redundancy; constituent replication \\

\textbf{Optimization and Reconfiguration} 
& Derive new valid system configurations under disturbance or resource constraints. 
& Multi-objective optimization (e.g., NSGA-II); dynamic task redistribution; architecture reconfiguration; reinforcement learning-based adaptation \\

\textbf{Estimation and Prediction} 
& Address uncertainty by inferring system state and modeling future behavior. 
& Kalman filters; neural networks (e.g., LSTM); time-series forecasting \\
