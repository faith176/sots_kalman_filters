\textbf{Belongs}
& \textbf{Stable participation.} 
\newline The system participates and delivers its capabilities as intended.
& \textbf{Impaired participation.} 
\newline The system participates but delivers incorrect or reduced capabilities, introducing disturbances for dependent systems. \\

\textbf{Does not belong}
& \textbf{Structural withdrawal.} 
\newline The system is capable but absent from the SoTS; its absence may disrupt systems that depend on it.
& \textbf{External disturbance.} 
\newline The system is absent and impaired; instability remains outside the SoTS but may affect it if the system later participates. \\
