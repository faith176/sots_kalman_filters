\toprule
\textbf{Level} & \textbf{Key Idea} & \textbf{What Changes from Previous Level} \\
\midrule

Level 1: Reactive 
& Respond to disturbances after they occur 
& No restrictions on participation. Systems join or leave freely; the system only observes and announces changes. \\

Level 2: Proactive 
& Prepare for disturbances before they occur 
& Introduces a short stabilization period during entry and exit. Systems still join or leave, but the system announces these changes in advance so others can prepare. \\

Level 3: Controlled 
& Prevent disturbance-causing situations 
& Introduces control over entry and exit. Systems no longer join or leave freely; the system regulates these transitions to avoid disruption. \\

Level 4: Adaptive 
& Adapt to disturbances during operation 
& Introduces adaptation based on system condition. The system compensates for degraded constituents, allowing them to continue contributing instead of leaving immediately. \\
