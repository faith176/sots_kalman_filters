\toprule
\textbf{Level} & \textbf{Key Idea} & \textbf{What Changes from Previous Level} \\
\midrule

Level 1: Reactive 
& Respond to disturbances after they occur 
& No restrictions on participation. Systems freely join or leave; changes are only observed and announced. \\

Level 2: Proactive 
& Prepare for disturbances before they occur 
& Adds a short stabilization period during entry and exit. Systems still join or leave immediately, but these changes are announced in advance so others can prepare. \\

Level 3: Controlled 
& Prevent disturbance-causing situations 
& Adds more control over when systems can join or leave. Systems can no longer enter or exit freely; these changes are regulated to avoid disruption. \\

Level 4: Adaptive 
& Adapt to disturbances during operation 
& Adds adaptation based on system condition. Systems can be compensated for and continue contributing instead of immediately leaving. \\
