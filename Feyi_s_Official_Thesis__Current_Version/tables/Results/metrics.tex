\textbf{Prediction Accuracy} &
Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) &
Measure the reconstruction error between the observed and predicted sensor values:
\[
\text{MAE} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} | \hat{x}_i - x_i |, 
\qquad
\text{RMSE} = \sqrt{ \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (\hat{x}_i - x_i)^2 }.
\]
Lower values indicate more accurate compensation during data loss and directly quantify reconstruction performance. \\

\addlinespace[0.4em]
\textbf{Event Detection Performance} &
Precision, Recall, F1-score &
Quantify how accurately the CEP engine detects true complex events from both observed and reconstructed inputs: 
$\text{Precision} = \tfrac{TP}{TP + FP}$, 
$\text{Recall} = \tfrac{TP}{TP + FN}$, 
$\text{F1} = 2 \cdot \tfrac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}}$.


F1 captures the balance between false alarms and missed detections, providing a compact measure of event reasoning correctness. \\



\addlinespace[0.4em]
\textbf{Event Timing Accuracy} &
Average Event Delay (AED) &
Evaluates how closely the detected or reconstructed events occur relative to their expected timestamps.
Defined as 
$\mathrm{AED} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_i |t_i^{\text{detected}} - t_i^{\text{expected}}|$.
An average delay under a defined threshold (e.g., $1\,\mathrm{s}$) indicates real-time stability. Large deviations imply delayed or missed event detection. \\

\addlinespace[0.4em]
\textbf{Confidence–Error Correlation} &
Pearson Correlation ($\rho_{c,e}$) &
Assesses the calibration between reconstruction confidence and actual reconstruction error. Computed as the correlation between predicted confidence and absolute error across all samples.
A strong negative correlation ($\rho_{c,e} \approx -1$) indicates that confidence estimates are well aligned with prediction reliability. \\
