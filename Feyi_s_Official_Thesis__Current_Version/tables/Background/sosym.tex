\textbf{Dimension} & 
\textbf{System} & 
\textbf{System of Systems} & 
\textbf{System of Twinned Systems} &
\textbf{Cross References}\\
\hline

Autonomy &
Subsystems cede autonomy to centralized control. &
Constituent systems retain autonomy while contributing to overall SoS goals. &
Each twin maintains autonomy over its data, models, and analytics, enabling self-managed operation and cooperation. &
\textbf{System:} Passive Parts \cite{jackson1984towards}; Autonomy \cite{boardman2006system}.\newline
\textbf{SoS:} Operational/Managerial Independence \cite{maier1998architecting}; Autonomy \cite{boardman2006system}; Autonomy \cite{nielsen2015systems}; Purposeful Parts \cite{jackson1984towards}.\newline
\textbf{SoTS:} Goals and Conflict in Coalition \cite{borth2019digital}; Autonomy Capabilities \cite{dietz2020digital}. \\

\hline

Independence &
Subsystems depend fully on the whole for purpose and operation. &
Constituent systems remain operationally and managerially independent. &
Assets and twins are independently governed but cooperate through DT-level interoperability and model alignment. &
\textbf{System:} No Individual Goals \cite{jackson1984towards}; Non Purposeful Elements \cite{ackoff1971towards}.\newline
\textbf{SoS:} Operational/Managerial Independence \cite{maier1998architecting}; Independence \cite{nielsen2015systems}.\newline
\textbf{SoTS:} Reflection \cite{borth2019digital}; Enterprise Constituent Autonomy \cite{dietz2020digital}. \\

\hline

Distribution &
Components are co-located under single management. &
Constituent systems are geographically and organizationally distributed. &
Distribution spans cyber--physical--digital layers; twins link physically separated systems via synchronized virtual representations. &
\textbf{System:} Closed System \cite{ackoff1971towards}; Connectivity \cite{boardman2006system}.\newline
\textbf{SoS:} Geographic Distribution \cite{maier1998architecting}; Distribution \cite{nielsen2015systems}; Connectivity \cite{boardman2006system}.\newline
\textbf{SoTS:} Horizontal/Vertical Integration \cite{michael2022integration}; Digital Thread \cite{borth2019digital}; Cross Domain \cite{dietz2020digital} \\

\hline

Evolution &
System evolution is centrally planned. &
SoS evolve continuously as systems join, leave, or change roles. &
Twins co-evolve with assets; data, models, and analytics update alongside system changes. &
\textbf{System:} Closed Environment \cite{jackson1984towards}; Static (One State) \cite{ackoff1971towards}; Structural/Static \cite{jordan1960some}.\newline
\textbf{SoS:} Evolutionary Development \cite{maier1998architecting}; Evolution \cite{nielsen2015systems}; Open Environment \cite{jackson1984towards}; Functional/Dynamic \cite{jordan1960some}.\newline
\textbf{SoTS:} Lifecycle Continuity \cite{michael2022integration}; Co-Evolution \cite{olsson2023systems}; Digital Thread \cite{borth2019digital}; Continuous Learning \cite{dietz2020digital}; Self Improvement \cite{esterle2021digital}. \\

\hline

Reconfiguration &
Occurs only through planned design changes. &
Systems dynamically change roles, participation, or contributions as goals evolve. &
Reconfiguration enabled through distributed DT decision-making using prescriptive analytics, simulation, and forecasting. &
\textbf{System:} Fully Observable \cite{jackson1984towards}; Mechanical System \cite{jordan1960some}.\newline
\textbf{SoS:} Dynamic Reconfiguration \cite{boardman2006system}; Reconfiguration \cite{nielsen2015systems}; Stable Intermediate Forms \cite{maier1998architecting}; Partially Observable \cite{jackson1984towards}; Organismic System \cite{jordan1960some}.\newline
\textbf{SoTS:} Continuous Reconfiguration \cite{gill2024toward}; Transient Relations \cite{dietz2020digital}; Dynamic Integration \cite{esterle2021digital}. \\

\hline

Emergence &
Emergent behavior is minimal or predictable. &
SoS exhibit emergent behavior from interactions among independent systems. &
Emergence arises through cyber–physical–digital feedback loops where twin predictions affect real-world dynamics. &
\textbf{System:} Variety Decreasing \cite{ackoff1971towards}; Emergence \cite{boardman2006system}.\newline
\textbf{SoS:} Emergent Behavior \cite{maier1998architecting}; Emergence \cite{boardman2006system}; Emergence \cite{nielsen2015systems}; Probabilistic Actions \cite{jackson1984towards}; Gestalt \cite{boulding1956general}.\newline
\textbf{SoTS:} Composition \cite{gill2024toward}; Autonomously Cooperating \cite{dietz2020digital}. \\

\hline

Interdependence &
Subsystems tightly coupled by fixed dependencies. &
Systems depend on one another’s capabilities while remaining independent. &
Twins exchange models, inferred states, and constraints, making one twin’s updates influence others. &
\textbf{System:} Connected by Distinguishable Principle \cite{jordan1960some}; Belonging \cite{boardman2006system}.\newline
\textbf{SoS:} Interdependence \cite{nielsen2015systems}; Belonging \cite{boardman2006system}.\newline
\textbf{SoTS:} Loose Coupling \cite{borth2019digital}; Mutal/Indirect Interaction  \cite{esterle2021digital}. \\

\hline

Interoperability &
Limited to standardized internal organizational protocols. &
Achieved through shared communication standards. &
Enabled by semantic, syntactic, and dynamic DT interoperability for autonomous coordination and cross-model reasoning. &
\textbf{System:} Diversity \cite{boardman2006system}.\newline
\textbf{SoS:} Diversity \cite{boardman2006system}; Interoperability \cite{nielsen2015systems}.\newline
\textbf{SoTS:} Semantic Alignment \cite{gill2024toward}; Heterogenous Interfaces \cite{michael2022integration}; Twin Interoperability \cite{olsson2023systems}; Semantic Interoperability \cite{borth2019digital}. \\

\hline
